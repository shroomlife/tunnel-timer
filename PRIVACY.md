# Privacy Policy for Tunnel Timer

Last updated: 10 November 2026

Tunnel Timer ("Tunnel Timer: Meeting Countdown" in the Microsoft Store) is a countdown app for Windows 11 published by shroomlife. This policy explains what the app does with your data. The short version: it keeps everything on your PC.

## What the app stores

The app stores the following on your device only, inside its own app data folder:

- The meetings you enter: optional title, start time and optional meeting link.
- The position of the countdown widget on your screen.
- Whether you turned on "Start with Windows".

A meeting is removed from storage when you acknowledge it, delete it, or two hours after its start time. Uninstalling the app removes all of this data.

## What the app does not do

- It does not collect, transmit or sell personal information.
- It has no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no telemetry and no advertising.
- It makes no network requests of its own.
- It does not read your calendar, your contacts or any files outside its own app data folder.

## Meeting links

If you add a link to a meeting and press "Join meeting", the app asks Windows to open that link in your default browser. From that moment the website or service behind the link (for example Zoom) applies its own privacy policy. Tunnel Timer does not send anything else along with the link.

## Microsoft Store

If you install the app from the Microsoft Store, Microsoft processes data about the download and may provide the publisher with aggregated, anonymous statistics such as installation counts and crash reports. That processing is governed by the [Microsoft Privacy Statement](https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement).

## Permissions

The app runs as a full trust desktop app (`runFullTrust`). This is required for any packaged Windows desktop app and is used to show the always-on-top widget and the notification area icon. It is not used to access personal data.
